This was also part of the “high-speed” course series before the start of actual machine learning courses. I hadn't studied probability since the second year of my undergraduate studies (so nothing in 7 years). This was a bit tough to follow, especially the conditionals and joint distributions part.

The professor was really good and took their time to explain the concepts in depth, and he almost always seemed to know how to explain things (which I now find is a very impressive skill/art).

Overall, really nice course, and it was fun to do the practice questions in class. Also no exam for this one :D
