This was a high-speed refresher course covering all the linear algebra topics we needed for machine learning. I felt it was a bit too fast, and I sometimes zoned out, but by the end, it was a good refresher. 

The course not only refreshed linear algebra but also covered critical mathematical thinking, logic, reading proofs, and more.

There was also a final exam to this 3-week excursion, which came as a surprise to all of us who were thinking this was just for revision, mais voila, bon.
